## 🛰️ About Me

I am a passionate **Robotics and Automation Engineer** with a dual mastery of low-level embedded hardware and high-level autonomous robotic systems:

- 🔬 **Hardware & Firmware:** Designing custom schematics & multi-layer PCBs in **KiCad**, bare-metal & HAL firmware on **STM32 (ARM Cortex)** & **ESP32**, hardware bring-up via ST-Link/SWD, and signal integrity debugging using oscilloscopes and logic analyzers.
- 🚗 **Autonomous Robotics:** Autonomous ground vehicles (UGV) and aerial drones (UAV) leveraging **ROS 2 Humble**, **Nav2**, **Cartographer SLAM**, and **Gazebo** simulation.
- 📡 **Protocols & RF:** Low-latency, zero-loss telemetry across **915 MHz RFM69HCW** transceivers, industrial **Modbus**, **SPI**, **I2C**, **UART**, and **MQTT**.
- 👁️ **Computer Vision & AI:** Real-time object detection (**YOLOv8**), person re-identification, edge vision with **OpenCV**, and enterprise AI chatbot integration.
